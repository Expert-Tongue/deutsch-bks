Warum Persischsprachige Vorteile haben

You already know more German than you think

Persian and German are both Indo-European languages. Many basic words are related, Persian already puts the verb at the end (German does that too, half of the time), and Persian has the sounds خ and غ, which most learners of German find hard. This page turns those into shortcuts. Tap any to hear the German.

1 · Schrift · خط

Left to right, every vowel written, nouns with capitals

Left to rightGerman is written in Latin letters from left to right. از چپ به راست.
Every vowel is writtenPersian leaves out short vowels (کتاب). German writes all of them: read every letter. همه حروف صدادار نوشته می‌شوند.
Nouns start with a capitalder Tisch, die Wohnung, das Haus. Persian has no capitals, so this is a handy signal: a capital letter in the middle of a sentence = a noun.

3 · Satzbau · ترتیب کلمات

Persian: verb at the end. German: verb 2nd, the rest at the end

In Persian the verb always comes last. In a German main clause the conjugated verb is in position 2. But a second verb, a participle, or the verb after weil / dass goes to the end, exactly where Persian puts it.

Ich arbeite heute.من امروز کار می‌کنم.
Heute arbeite ich.Time first? The verb stays in position 2, the subject moves behind it.
Ich muss heute arbeiten.امروز باید کار کنم. (infinitive at the end, like Persian)
Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf.The prefix auf- jumps to the end. ساعت ۷ بلند می‌شوم.
Kommen Sie aus dem Iran?Yes/no question: verb first. Persian uses intonation or آیا: آیا اهل ایران هستید؟
Sie = شماFormal „Sie" works like your شما, but the verb is in the 3rd person plural: Sie sind (شما هستید), Sie haben. Always with a capital S.
4 · Artikel & Genus · جنس دستوری

Persian has no gender. German has three.

In Persian او means he and she, and nouns have no gender. German has der, die, das and er / sie / es. This is the biggest new habit: learn every noun with its article, and practise er (he) and sie (she) until they are automatic.

Teil 1 · Formular

Fill in the form for your friend

Ihr Freund Reza Ahmadi ist 32 Jahre alt und kommt aus Schiras im Iran. Er wohnt jetzt in der Königstraße 12, 70173 Stuttgart. Er ist Elektriker von Beruf. Er arbeitet tagsüber und möchte einen Deutschkurs am Abend machen.

Reza wants to register at the Volkshochschule. Write the 5 missing details. دوست شما رضا می‌خواهد در کلاس آلمانی ثبت‌نام کند. ۵ مورد خالی را پر کنید.

Achtung!German forms use Vorname (نام) and Familienname / Nachname (نام خانوادگی). The postcode (PLZ) comes before the city: 70173 Stuttgart. Dates are written day.month.year: 21.03.1994.
