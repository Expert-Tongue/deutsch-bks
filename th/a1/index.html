Was Thaisprachige schon können

Your head start: the same basic word order

Thai and German both start with who – does – what: Ich trinke Kaffee = ฉันดื่มกาแฟ. Many Thais also know some English, a sister language of German: Haus (house), Wasser (water). German has no tones: the melody of a word never changes its meaning. What is new: verb endings, articles (der, die, das), plural endings and cases. Thai shows these things with small words like แล้ว, จะ or ให้; German changes the words themselves. Tap any to hear the German.

1 · Aussprache · การออกเสียง

German sounds, written in Thai

Read the right-hand column as Thai, without tones. German stresses the first syllable of most words: Ar-beit, Fa-mi-lie is an exception (Fa-mi-lie).

GermanSounds likeExampleRead it as
Achtung!Say the last consonant, all of it. Thai words end only in -k, -t, -p, -n, -m, -ng, and they are not released. German words can end in -l, -s, -f, -sch and in groups of consonants: Herbst, Obst, Bus (not „bát"), Ball (not „ban"). Say every sound.
r and lIn German r and l are different words: Reis (rice) vs. Leis (quiet), frei vs. flieg. Keep the r from the throat, never as ล. And German has no tones: the voice only goes up at the end of a yes/no question.
2 · Satzbau · การเรียงประโยค

Same start as Thai, but the verb is always 2nd

Thai and German both say Ich arbeite heute = ฉันทำงานวันนี้. But in German the conjugated verb must stay in position 2, and a second verb goes to the end.

Ich arbeite heute.ฉันทำงานวันนี้
Heute arbeite ich.วันนี้ฉันทำงาน. Thai keeps ฉัน before the verb; German swaps: never „Heute ich arbeite".
Ich muss heute arbeiten.วันนี้ฉันต้องทำงาน. The second verb goes to the end.
Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf.ฉันตื่นนอนตอนเจ็ดโมง. „auf" jumps to the end.
Kommen Sie aus Thailand?คุณมาจากประเทศไทยใช่ไหม? No ไหม at the end: the verb goes first.
Verbs changeThai verbs never change (ฉันไป, เขาไป, พวกเขาไป). German verbs take endings: ich gehe, du gehst, er geht, wir gehen. Learn these four endings first: they work for most verbs.
Sie, du, ihrInstead of คุณ, เธอ, พี่, น้อง… and ครับ / ค่ะ, German has du (one friend), ihr (several friends) and Sie (polite, always with a capital S). Use Sie with everyone you would speak to with ครับ / ค่ะ at work or at an office.
3 · Artikel & Genus · เพศของคำนาม

der, die, das: think of them as classifiers

Thai uses classifiers: หนังสือสองเล่ม, แมวหนึ่งตัว, ครูสามคน. German puts an article before every noun: der Tisch, die Katze, das Buch. Just like a classifier, you must learn it with each noun. There are three: masculine, feminine and neuter, and they rarely follow logic. Nouns are always written with a capital letter.

PluralThai repeats the word (เด็ก ๆ) or adds a number; German changes the noun itself: das Kind → die Kinder, der Tisch → die Tische, die Frau → die Frauen, das Auto → die Autos. Learn the plural with the noun: der Tisch, die Tische.
4 · Fälle · การก

Who does what? German shows it with the article

Thai shows roles through word order and small words like ให้ and ของ. German also changes the article. At A1, focus on two cases: Nominativ (who does it) and Akkusativ (what or whom). Only der changes: der → den.

FallGermanThaiClue
Nominativder Mann ist hier.ผู้ชายคนนั้นอยู่ที่นี่who does it
AkkusativIch sehe den Mann.ฉันเห็นผู้ชายคนนั้นwhat / whom → den
DativIch gebe dem Mann das Buch.ฉันให้หนังสือแก่ผู้ชายคนนั้น„ให้ / แก่" → dem
Genitivdas Auto des Mannesรถของผู้ชายคนนั้น„ของ" → des
Your clueWhere Thai uses ให้ / แก่ (to someone), German uses the Dativ: dem, der, einem, mir, dir. Where Thai uses ของ, German uses the Genitiv, or simply „von": das Auto von Somchai = รถของสมชาย.

Lesen · การอ่าน

Reading practice

Scan for key words before you read the statement. Negations (nicht, kein, nur, geschlossen, verboten) are the most common traps.

Teil 1 · Formular

Fill in the form for your friend

Ihr Freund Somchai Wongsa ist 32 Jahre alt und kommt aus Chiang Mai in Thailand. Er wohnt jetzt in der Königstraße 12, 70173 Stuttgart. Er ist Elektriker von Beruf. Er arbeitet tagsüber und möchte einen Deutschkurs am Abend machen.

Somchai wants to register at the Volkshochschule. Write the 5 missing details. เพื่อนของคุณ สมชาย อยากสมัครเรียนภาษาเยอรมัน เขียนข้อมูลที่ขาดหายไป 5 อย่าง

Achtung!German forms use Vorname (ชื่อ) and Familienname / Nachname (นามสกุล). The postcode (PLZ) comes before the city: 70173 Stuttgart. Country: „aus Thailand", with no article. Write your real name, not your nickname, and spell Thai place names as in your passport: Chiang Mai, Khon Kaen. Dates are written day.month.year, as in Thailand, but with the Western year: 2026, not 2569.

Teil 1 · buchstabieren

Spell your name the German way

You will be asked to spell your name. Orange letters are traps for Thai speakers, who know the English letter names: A is „อา" (not „เอ"), E is „เอ" (not „อี"), I is „อี" (not „ไอ"), C is „ทเซ", G is „เก" (not „จี"), H is „ฮา" (not „เอช"), J is „ยอท" (not „เจ"), R is „แอร์" from the throat, U is „อู" (not „ยู"), V is „ฟาว", W is „เว", Y is „อึปซีลอน" and Z is „ทเซท". Tap a letter to hear it.
