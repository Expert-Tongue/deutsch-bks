Choose a mode

  • -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft → always die
  • -chen, -lein → always das
  • Don't trust the BKS gender: sunce is neuter, die Sonne is feminine.
  • Persian has no gender, so learn every noun with its article. Colours help: der blue, die red, das green.
  • Romanian has three genders too, but they often differ: die Sonne but soarele, der Mond but luna, das Haus but casa. And the article goes in front: der Mann = omul.
  • Hungarian has no grammatical gender (ő = he and she), so learn der, die or das with every German noun, as one word: der Tisch, die Lampe, das Buch. Shortcuts: -ung, -heit, -keit are die; -chen is das; every plural is die.
  • Greek works the same way: three genders, article in front (ο, η, το). But the genders often differ (το τραπέζι, but der Tisch), so every noun must be learned with its German article: der Tisch, die Lampe, das Buch. Shortcuts: -ung, -heit, -keit are die; -chen is das; every plural is die.
  • Bulgarian has three genders too, but they often don't match (маса is feminine, but der Tisch is masculine), and German puts the article in front, so every noun must be learned with it: der Tisch, die Lampe, das Buch. Shortcuts: -ung, -heit, -keit are die; -chen is das; every plural is die.
  • Albanian has two genders, German has three, and they often don't match (shtëpia is feminine, but das Haus is neuter).
