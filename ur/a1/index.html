Warum Urdusprachige Vorteile haben

You already know more German than you think

Urdu and German are distant cousins in the Indo-European family. Urdu puts the verb at the end (German does that too, half of the time), already has two grammatical genders, and uses the same "to me" structure as German (مجھے ٹھنڈ لگ رہی ہے = Mir ist kalt). Most Pakistani and Indian learners also know English, which helps with many words. Tap any to hear the German.

1 · Aussprache · تلفظ

German sounds, written in Urdu letters

German spelling often uses several letters for one sound. Read the right-hand column as Urdu and you will be close.

GermanSounds likeExampleRead it as
Achtung!t and d are dental in German, like Urdu ت and د, not ٹ and ڈ (which South Asian English often uses). And German p, t, k at the start of a word have a small puff of air, like Urdu پھ، تھ، کھ (the aspirated sounds), not like پ، ت، ک.
Achtung!w and v: German w is always a clear v sound (Wasser = واسر), and German v is usually f (Vater = فاتر). Two vowels Urdu doesn't have: ü (say ای with rounded lips) and ö (say اے with rounded lips).
2 · Satzbau · جملے کی ترتیب

Urdu: verb at the end. German: verb 2nd, the rest at the end

In Urdu the verb comes last. In a German main clause the conjugated verb is in position 2. But a second verb, a participle, or the verb after weil / dass goes to the end, exactly where Urdu puts it.

Ich arbeite heute.میں آج کام کرتا ہوں۔
Heute arbeite ich.Time first? The verb stays in position 2, the subject moves behind it.
Ich muss heute arbeiten.مجھے آج کام کرنا ہے۔ (infinitive at the end, like Urdu)
Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf.The prefix auf- jumps to the end. میں 7 بجے اٹھتا ہوں۔
Kommen Sie aus Pakistan?Yes/no question: verb first. Urdu uses کیا or intonation: کیا آپ پاکستان سے ہیں؟
Sie = آپFormal „Sie" works exactly like آپ: it takes a plural verb (Sie sind = آپ ہیں) and always has a capital S. Urdu has three levels (آپ، تم، تو); German has two. Use Sie with strangers, at work and at offices.
3 · Artikel & Genus · جنس

Urdu has two genders. German has three, and they don't match.

You already know that nouns have gender: لڑکا is masculine, روٹی is feminine. German adds a third, neuter (das), and the genders often differ from Urdu. Learn every noun with its article.

Good newsGerman verbs don't change for gender. Urdu says کھاتا / کھاتی; German says er isst, sie isst. Words ending in -ung, -heit, -keit are always die; -chen is always das.
4 · Fälle · حالتیں

Your کو and کا, in German

Urdu marks roles with postpositions after the noun (کو، کا، سے). German changes the article before the noun.

FallGermanUrduYour clue
Nominativder Mann ist hier.آدمی یہاں ہے۔who does it
AkkusativIch sehe den Mann.میں آدمی کو دیکھتا ہوں۔object → den
DativIch gebe dem Mann das Buch.میں آدمی کو کتاب دیتا ہوں۔کو (receiver) → dem
Genitivdas Auto des Mannesآدمی کی گاڑیکا / کی → des
Same as UrduGerman has "to me" sentences just like Urdu مجھے: Mir ist kalt (مجھے ٹھنڈ لگ رہی ہے), Das gefällt mir (یہ مجھے پسند ہے), Mir geht es gut (میں ٹھیک ہوں). mir = مجھے.

Teil 1 · Formular

Fill in the form for your friend

Ihr Freund Bilal Ahmed ist 32 Jahre alt und kommt aus Lahore in Pakistan. Er wohnt jetzt in der Königstraße 12, 70173 Stuttgart. Er ist Elektriker von Beruf. Er arbeitet tagsüber und möchte einen Deutschkurs am Abend machen.

Bilal wants to register at the Volkshochschule. Write the 5 missing details. آپ کا دوست بلال جرمن کورس میں داخلہ لینا چاہتا ہے۔ باقی 5 معلومات بھریں۔

Achtung!German forms use Vorname (پہلا نام) and Familienname / Nachname (خاندانی نام). The postcode (PLZ) comes before the city: 70173 Stuttgart. Dates are written day.month.year: 14.08.1995, as in Pakistan.

Teil 1 · buchstabieren

Spell your name the German way

You will be asked to spell your name. Orange letters are traps if you learned the English alphabet: A is „a" (not ay), E is „e", I is „i", H is „ha" (not aitch), J is „jot", R is „er", V is „fau", W is „ve", Y is „üpsilon", Z is „tset". Tap a letter to hear it.
