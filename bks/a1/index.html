Warum BKS-Sprecher schneller sind

You already know more German than you think

Speakers of Bosnian, Croatian and Serbian start with real advantages: a phonetic spelling habit, a case system with seven cases, and hundreds of German loanwords from the Austro-Hungarian era. This page turns those into shortcuts. Tap any to hear the German.

1 · Aussprache · izgovor

German sounds, written with your letters

German spelling uses several letters for one sound. Read the right-hand column as you would read BKS and you will be close.

GermanSounds likeExampleRead it as
Pazi!German has sounds you don't have. ü: say „i" with rounded lips. ö: say „e" with rounded lips. ch after e/i (ich, nicht): a soft hiss between š and h, not „ih". And German devoices final consonants: Tag sounds like „tak", und like „unt".
2 · Fälle · padeži

English speakers find German cases hard. You don't, because the idea is the same as your padeži. The difference: in BKS the noun ending changes, in German mostly the article changes.

6 · Satzbau · red riječi

The verb has a fixed place

In BKS you can move words around freely. German is strict: the conjugated verb is always in position 2. A second verb or a separable prefix goes to the very end.

Heute arbeite ich.Danas radim. / Radim danas. (verb stays 2nd)
Ich muss heute arbeiten.Moram danas raditi. (second verb at the end)
Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf.Ustajem u 7 sati. (prefix auf- jumps to the end)
Kommen Sie aus Bosnien?Jeste li iz Bosne? (yes/no question: verb first)
Sie = ViFormal „Sie" works like your „Vi", but the verb is in the 3rd person plural: Sie sind (vi ste), Sie haben (vi imate). Always with a capital S.

Hören · slušanje

Listening practice

Read the question first, then press „Anhören". Your browser reads the text in German. Use „Text" only after you have answered. In Teil 2 you hear the text only once in the real exam: focus on numbers and on nicht / kein.

Lesen · čitanje

Reading practice

Scan for key words before you read the statement. Negations (nicht, kein, nur, geschlossen, verboten) are the most common traps.

Teil 1 · Formular

Fill in the form for your friend

Ihr Freund Edin Mujić ist 32 Jahre alt und kommt aus Tuzla in Bosnien und Herzegowina. Er wohnt jetzt in der Königstraße 12, 70173 Stuttgart. Er ist Elektriker von Beruf. Er arbeitet tagsüber und möchte einen Deutschkurs am Abend machen.

Edin wants to register at the Volkshochschule. Write the 5 missing details. (BKS: Vaš prijatelj Edin se želi upisati na tečaj njemačkog. Upišite 5 podataka koji nedostaju.)

Pazi!German forms use Vorname (ime) and Familienname / Nachname (prezime). The postcode (PLZ) comes before the city: 70173 Stuttgart.
