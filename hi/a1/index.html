Warum Hindisprachige Vorteile haben

You already know more German than you think

Hindi and German are distant cousins in the Indo-European family. Hindi puts the verb at the end (German does that too, half of the time), already has two grammatical genders, and uses the same "to me" structure as German (मुझे ठंड लग रही है = Mir ist kalt). Most Indian learners also know English, which helps with many words. Tap any to hear the German.

1 · Aussprache · उच्चारण

German sounds, written in Devanagari

German spelling often uses several letters for one sound. Read the right-hand column as Hindi and you will be close.

GermanSounds likeExampleRead it as
Achtung!t and d are dental in German, like Hindi त and द, not retroflex ट and ड (which Indian English often uses). And German p, t, k at the start of a word have a small puff of air, like Hindi फ, थ, ख (the aspirated sounds), not like प, त, क.
Achtung!w and v: German w is always a clear v sound (Wasser = वासर), and German v is usually f (Vater = फ़ाटर). Two vowels Hindi doesn't have: ü (say ई with rounded lips) and ö (say ए with rounded lips).
2 · Satzbau · वाक्य क्रम

Hindi: verb at the end. German: verb 2nd, the rest at the end

In Hindi the verb comes last. In a German main clause the conjugated verb is in position 2. But a second verb, a participle, or the verb after weil / dass goes to the end, exactly where Hindi puts it.

Ich arbeite heute.मैं आज काम करता हूँ।
Heute arbeite ich.Time first? The verb stays in position 2, the subject moves behind it.
Ich muss heute arbeiten.मुझे आज काम करना है। (infinitive at the end, like Hindi)
Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf.The prefix auf- jumps to the end. मैं 7 बजे उठता हूँ।
Kommen Sie aus Indien?Yes/no question: verb first. Hindi uses क्या or intonation: क्या आप भारत से हैं?
Sie = आपFormal „Sie" works exactly like आप: it takes a plural verb (Sie sind = आप हैं) and always has a capital S. Hindi has three levels (आप, तुम, तू); German has two. Use Sie with strangers, at work and at offices.
3 · Artikel & Genus · लिंग

Hindi has two genders. German has three, and they don't match.

You already know that nouns have gender: लड़का is masculine, रोटी is feminine. German adds a third, neuter (das), and the genders often differ from Hindi. Learn every noun with its article.

Good newsGerman verbs don't change for gender. Hindi says खाता / खाती; German says er isst, sie isst. Words ending in -ung, -heit, -keit are always die; -chen is always das.
4 · Fälle · कारक

Your को and का, in German

Hindi marks roles with postpositions after the noun (को, का, से). German changes the article before the noun.

FallGermanHindiYour clue
Nominativder Mann ist hier.आदमी यहाँ है।who does it

DativIch gebe dem Mann das Buch.मैं आदमी को किताब देता हूँ।को (receiver) → dem

Same as HindiGerman has "to me" sentences just like Hindi मुझे: Mir ist kalt (मुझे ठंड लग रही है), Das gefällt mir (यह मुझे पसंद है), Mir geht es gut (मैं ठीक हूँ). mir = मुझे.

Teil 1 · Formular

Fill in the form for your friend

Ihr Freund Rahul Verma ist 32 Jahre alt und kommt aus Pune in Indien. Er wohnt jetzt in der Königstraße 12, 70173 Stuttgart. Er ist Elektriker von Beruf. Er arbeitet tagsüber und möchte einen Deutschkurs am Abend machen.

Rahul wants to register at the Volkshochschule. Write the 5 missing details. आपका दोस्त राहुल जर्मन कोर्स में दाख़िला लेना चाहता है। बाक़ी 5 जानकारियाँ भरें।

Achtung!German forms use Vorname (पहला नाम) and Familienname / Nachname (उपनाम). The postcode (PLZ) comes before the city: 70173 Stuttgart. Dates are written day.month.year: 15.08.1995, as in India.
